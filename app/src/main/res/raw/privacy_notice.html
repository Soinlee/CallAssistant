Privacy Notice

CallAssistant is fully offline. This app does not connect to the internet, and does not collect, upload, or transmit any of your personal data to any server.

All features work entirely on your device:

• No network requests: The app declares no internet permission and never makes a network connection.

• Local data only: Call logs, marks, settings, and all other data are stored and processed locally on this device.

• No account, no tracking: No registration, no analytics, no advertising SDKs.

Permissions are used only for local features you enable, such as reading the current call number and showing the caller-info floating window. None of this data leaves your device.
